HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Moveset 1: W / A / S / D to move

Moveset 2: Arrow Keys to move

W
A S D

GAMEPLAY

  • Avoid vehicles on the roads! Getting hit means GAME OVER.
  • Use wood logs to cross rivers. Falling into water means GAME OVER.
  • Collect 'Superhero-figured' items for bonus points.
  • Reach the top of the screen to advance to the next level.

OTHER KEYS

P / Esc - Pause the game

S - Save your progress

R - Restart (when game over)

Q - Quit to menu
